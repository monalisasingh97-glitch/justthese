Privacy Policy

Just These · Last updated 17 September 2026

The short version: Just These collects nothing. It has no account system, no analytics, no advertising, and no networking code of any kind, so your photos and settings cannot leave your device.

Information we collect

None. Just These has no servers, and we have no way to receive information from your device. We cannot see your photos, your PIN, how often you use the app, or that you have installed it at all.

Photo library access

The app asks for access to your photo library so it can display your own photos for you to choose from, and show the chosen ones in Handover Mode. Photos are read for display only. They are never copied, modified, deleted, uploaded, or transmitted. Your selection is a list of references to photos already on your device, and it is cleared as soon as a handover ends.

Your PIN

Your PIN is never stored. Only a salted SHA-256 hash of it is saved in the app's own local storage, which cannot be reversed to recover the PIN. It never leaves your device. If you enable device unlock, Face ID and Touch ID are handled entirely by iOS — the app is only told whether authentication succeeded, and never receives your biometric data or device passcode.

Local storage

The app saves a small amount of data on your device: your PIN hash, whether device unlock is enabled, whether the app lock is enabled, and whether you have seen the tips. Deleting the app removes all of it.

Third parties

There are none. Just These contains no analytics SDK, no advertising SDK, no crash reporter, and no third-party service of any kind. No data is shared or sold, because none is collected.

Children

Just These is suitable for all ages and collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of the app changes what it accesses, this page will be updated before that version ships, and the date at the top will change.
